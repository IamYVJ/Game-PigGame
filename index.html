How to Play

Pig is a two-player turn-based dice game. First to reach 100 points wins.

  1. On your turn, press 🎲 Roll dice as many times as you like. Each roll (2–6) is added to your current score.
  2. Roll a 1 and you lose your current score — your turn passes to the other player.
  3. Press 📥 Hold to bank your current score into your total and end your turn safely.
  4. Press 🔄 New game to reset and start over.

How Optimal Play Works

With the toggle on, the indicator tells the active player whether to roll or hold — picking the move that gives the highest chance of winning the game, not just the most points this turn.

  1. Every situation is captured by three numbers: your score, your opponent's score, and your current turn total.
  2. For each one we work out your probability of eventually winning if you roll versus if you hold, allowing for the 1-in-6 chance of busting on a 1.
  3. Those probabilities depend on one another, so they're solved by value iteration: every state is refined over and over until the numbers stop changing.
  4. The indicator then recommends whichever action has the higher win probability and shows that percentage.

This is the game-theoretically optimal strategy described by Neller & Presser. A fun consequence: with perfect play, the player who goes first wins about 53% of the time.
